TITLE: Between Duty and Desire
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 4089

Orion staggered into the training grounds, soaked and gasping for air. This was where he had trained with Arannis on the first day, but now it was empty. Panic set in as he realized no one was there. He wondered if Calypso and Lorelei had escaped or been captured. He shouted Calypso’s name into the storm, but the thunder drowned him out. The ground had turned to sludge, the rain intensifying, frustration boiling over as he yelled again.
[[File:Orion fills Lorelei and Calypso in on whats happened .png|thumb|Orion fills Lorelei and Calypso in on whats happened ]]
Relief washed over him when Calypso answered from nearby shelter. She and Lorelei had realized the sound was not thunder. Calypso scolded him for standing in the rain and pulled him closer. Orion could barely catch his breath, overwhelmed that they were alive. Calypso urged him to change out of his soaked clothes, but he cut her off. There was no time. 

He told them everything. Hyril’ar was under attack from within. A coup. Sylax had turned much of the royal guard and launched coordinated strikes across the realm. Orion did not know how many had betrayed the crown, only that it was bad. Worse, they were coming for Calypso. She was the only thing in their way.

Calypso reeled, admitting she had been warned this might happen someday, just not now. Orion insisted they could not return to the palace. Lorelei argued that the guard answered to her father, Arannis, and he would never betray Calypso. Orion told her the truth. Arannis had been betrayed. He was the one who ordered Orion to warn them and get them out. When the attack began, Arannis stayed behind to buy time.

Lorelei panicked for her father, but Orion reminded her that Arannis was too valuable to be executed outright and too strong to fall easily. If they were captured too, there would be no way to help him. Orion had promised to get them to safety.

The weight finally crushed Calypso. She collapsed, trembling, anger and disbelief spilling out in muttered curses. Orion watched her struggle, seeing her shaken in a way he never had before. She spoke of broken oaths, of traitors, of not knowing who to trust. Orion reminded her of Arannis’s faith in her, and told her he believed in her as well.

That steadied her. Calypso rose, reclaiming herself, declaring they would move immediately. She sent Lorelei to gather supplies. As Lorelei left, a portal opened behind them. Orion recognized it, suggesting Ekabar so they could regroup. Calypso stopped him. The portal was only for him. She refused to risk being trapped away from Hyril’ar again. She would not abandon her people.

Orion argued, but knew her resolve. He instead passed on Arannis’s guidance, directions to Vael’Thalas Hold and the families who would never betray the crown. Calypso listened, thanked him quietly, and told him to go. He refused. If she stayed, so would he. They would face this together.

The tension softened. Calypso admitted the burden was crushing, that her father had ruled for centuries without faltering while she nearly lost everything in less than a decade. Orion told her it was not her fault, that strength was often mistaken for luck or fate. She questioned why he cared so deeply. He told her her war became his the moment she became part of his life.

She admitted she would leave everything behind if she could, but she could not abandon Hyril’ar. Still, she thanked him for showing her another way to live. For warmth, laughter, and moments that made life feel real. She promised that when this was over, she would walk away from the crown and return to him.
[[File:Calypso kisses Orion.png|thumb|Calypso kisses Orion]]
She stepped forward and kissed him, deep and breathless, as if time stopped. When she pulled back, Orion tried to follow and realized he could not move. She had done this deliberately. Calm and almost playful, she spoke of broken taboos and consequences she no longer feared. She made him promise to not die on her. 

Before he could stop her, she sent him into the portal. Orion screamed her name as the light swallowed him.
