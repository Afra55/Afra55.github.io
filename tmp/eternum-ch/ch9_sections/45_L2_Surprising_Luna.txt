TITLE: Surprising Luna
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2038

Orion and Chang stepped out of the office with the tension still clinging to them. They shared a quick high five and split up without a word. Relief washed over Orion as it sank in that they had actually pulled it off. Nova’s malware was planted and running, and now it was just a matter of waiting for the data to roll in. For the first time, they were ahead of Bardot and the Founder. 

Thunder rumbled through Kredon’s financial district as dark clouds gathered overhead. Another storm was coming, and Orion picked up the pace. Success or not, his focus had already shifted. He had a date. 
[[File:Luna looking hella cute.png|thumb|Orion meets up with Luna]]
He spotted Luna nearby, and the sight of her instantly eased him. She looked beautiful, and her relief was obvious when he told her the plan had worked perfectly and no one suspected a thing. She admitted she had been worried, but Orion made sure she knew she had nailed her part and that the mission had only succeeded because everyone did their job. 

With the weather turning, Orion suggested they get moving. A brief misunderstanding made Luna think he was about to leave for the gym, but he quickly laughed it off and reassured her that he wasn’t going anywhere. He had set the entire day aside just for her, and the idea that she might have thought otherwise clearly bothered her more than she wanted to admit.

Once the moment passed, Luna asked where they were headed. Orion told her they were going to Kredon Station to catch a vintage themed train that only ran a few times a year. The realization that it was the Cascadian Express lit her up instantly. Orion explained the route, a long scenic loop through Cascade National Park and into Canada, and her excitement made the effort feel completely worth it.

With only twenty minutes before departure, Orion urged her to hurry. Luna moved ahead with a bright smile, calling back to him as she went. Watching her like that, more relaxed and cheerful around him, Orion couldn’t help but smile as he followed after her.
