TITLE: A Quiet Train Ride Together
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1756

[[File:Luna on the train.png|thumb|Luna on the train]]
After the conductor checked the ticket, Luna was amazed to realize they had an entire cabin to themselves. Orion confirmed it was all theirs, and the moment felt unreal to her. She thanked him, calling it a dream come true, while he brushed it off as just a train ride, though he explained the line had once been a major route before being rebuilt for tourism. Luna said she wished she could travel like this all the time. Even with the rain hiding some of the views, she loved the atmosphere, saying it made everything feel more magical, which Orion was happy to hear. She joked about a mystery happening on the train, but he preferred a peaceful trip. Luna then told him how special the day felt, especially knowing how busy he had been, and admitted that having his time meant more to her than he probably realized. Orion promised many more days like this, and she laughed, opening up about how loneliness had shaped her past and how new moments like this still felt. 

Luna mentioned her gloves and how taking them off felt like a small kind of freedom. Orion understood and encouraged her, and with his reassurance, she decided to take them off. Soon after, the conductor returned, introduced herself, and offered drinks. Orion ordered two hot chocolates without hesitation. Once alone again, he suggested watching a movie and picked a short horror film he had clearly planned ahead of time. Luna was impressed and intrigued, especially when she noticed it was a foreign film. As the movie began, Luna relaxed beside him, and when Orion gently took her hand, she leaned into him. They settled together quietly as the film played, the sound blending with the soft rain tapping against the windows.
