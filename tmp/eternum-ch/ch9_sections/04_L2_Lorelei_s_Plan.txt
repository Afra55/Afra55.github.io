TITLE: Lorelei's Plan
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1735

Back in his cell, Orion sank against the cold stone wall, dread settling in. He hated the thought of spending Christmas locked away and felt helpless without his powers. More than anything, he wished he could let the girls know where he was.

A small rat squeezed through the bars. Orion greeted it absently, joking and offering scraps of food—until it transformed into Lorelei. Shocked, he barely reacted before she silenced him, warning her presence was dangerous.

Relief came when she admitted she’d come to help—but escape was impossible without months of advanced magic training. Her real reason: curiosity and a warning. The Council had decided Orion would be executed in three days.

Orion panicked, arguing the Princess would never allow it, only to learn Calypso remained unconscious and dangerously ill. Guilt crushed him. Lorelei explained the Princess’s isolation under strict rules. Orion clung to hope, but admitted that if Calypso didn’t wake soon, it might already be too late.

Lorelei offered alternatives: convince her father to exile Orion for decades, or use a forbidden remedy hidden in the forest that might wake the Princess, though the cost would be severe if discovered.

Doubting herself, Lorelei asked if Orion could be trusted. He swore his innocence and devotion to Calypso. After a long pause, she chose to trust him, promising to return within three days.
[[File:Rat-Lorelei waving Goodbye.png|thumb|Rat-Lorelei waving Goodbye]]
Their conversation was cut short by a guard. Lorelei transformed back into a rat just in time. Orion feigned harmless muttering until the guard left. Alone again, he sank to the floor. Three days stretched ahead like an eternity, and all he could do was think of the girls.
