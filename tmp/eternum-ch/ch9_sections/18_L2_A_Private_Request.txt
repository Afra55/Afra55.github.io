TITLE: A Private Request
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 4404

[[File:Calypso and Lorelei show up in Orion's Chambers.png|thumb|Calypso and Lorelei show up in Orion's Chambers]]
Orion sat near the fire, letting the warmth sink in as night settled over Hyril’ar. The cold always seemed sharper once the sun went down, and it left him wondering how this world even measured time. Seasons, months, days. Everything here still felt foreign, and it only reminded him how much he did not understand yet. He caught himself thinking that once the Gem War was over, he wanted to return properly with the girls and truly learn this place. The thought drifted into a half joking idea about whether being the head of Ulysses might earn him some kind of standing at court, before a knock pulled him out of it. 

Lorelei’s voice came from the door, followed by Calypso herself when Orion invited them in. They entered quickly and shut the door behind them. Orion froze for a moment, struck by Calypso’s appearance. Her night robes were far less formal than her usual attire, light and sheer in places, clearly chosen for comfort rather than ceremony. She explained that their discussions had run long and that she had changed after returning to her chambers, removing most of her jewelry while keeping her catalyst bracers. When she asked if it made him uncomfortable, Orion assured her it did not and even complimented her, earning a shy thanks in return.

Curious and a little anxious, Orion asked how things had gone with the suitors. Calypso admitted the conversations had gone smoothly enough but that she had felt no real connection with any of them. Orion hid his relief poorly, though he quickly thanked her instead for allowing him to stand by her side during the contest. Calypso told him his presence had brought her unexpected comfort, which was why she hesitated before admitting she needed one last favor before he returned home. She promised it would be the final thing she ever asked of him and that it would not take long.

As she paced the room, visibly nervous, Calypso stopped and made him swear that whatever she said would remain secret, for the sake of the entire realm. Orion agreed without hesitation, sensing the weight behind her words. She then began speaking carefully, explaining that while humans were known for strong impulses, elves shared the same desires but practiced far stricter discipline. Their low birth rate, she said, was not due to lack of desire but to biology, a consequence of their long lives. Orion struggled to follow where this was going, his thoughts racing ahead of reason.

Calypso cut herself off and returned to the real issue. Her magic, though stronger than when she first awoke after the battle with Thanatos, had become unstable over time. Simple spells still came easily, but greater ones now slipped from her grasp. The Grand Druid believed this imbalance came from unreleased inner energy, and that she needed to find release to restore harmony. When Orion finally understood what she meant, he panicked, only for Calypso to snap him back to attention and make it clear she was not proposing anything improper. No human would ever touch her, and she was already bending rules simply by standing before him as she was.

What she asked instead was far simpler. Alone, she could not focus long enough to reach the state she needed, her thoughts constantly drifting. Lorelei had suggested that visual assistance might help her concentrate. Orion slowly realized what that meant just as Calypso confirmed it. Lorelei, flustered and rambling, tried to justify the idea by insisting it was purely for the Princess’s sake and entirely academic, even as her own curiosity betrayed her. Orion stopped her, saying he understood.

Calypso reminded him that he was free to refuse, that this was not an order but a request made in trust, between equals bound by what they had already shared. Orion stood there in disbelief, stunned that such a moment was even real. In the end, he accepted. He told her simply that he would help, just as he always had.

Calypso laid back and undressed both Orion and Lorelei with her magic. Lorelei and Orion shared a romantic moment but Calypso was left unsatisfied. Lorelei was told to leave as Calypso said she reached release. Orion knew Calypso was lying and called her out. Orion convinced Calypso to help her and she gave in. In the end, thanks to Orion, Calypso was finally able to release.
