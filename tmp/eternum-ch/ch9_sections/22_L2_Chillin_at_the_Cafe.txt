TITLE: Chillin at the Cafe
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1889

[[File:Luna at the cafe with Orionlsdhf.png|thumb|Luna at the cafe with Orion]]
A few days later, Orion sits on a café terrace, scrolling through the news and checking the balance in his uWallet. Eternum fills nearly every headline, and he finds himself wondering whether people truly do not know what is happening or simply choose not to. Luna joins him after breakfast, and they spend a quiet moment enjoying the rare sunlight and easy conversation. As they get ready to leave, she mentions her family visiting for the holidays and jokingly explains how her [[Mr. Hernandez|Father]] and [[Hugo|Uncle]] almost went into full panic mode after thinking Orion had upset her, only for the whole thing to be cleared up once they realized she had just been emotional from seeing him again. It is awkward in hindsight, but also strangely comforting to know how fiercely they look out for her. 
[[File:Changs Aura as he enters the cafe.png|thumb|Changs Aura as he enters the cafe]]
After Luna heads off, Orion reflects on how much she held everyone together while he was gone, even when she was scared herself. He wants to do something special for her, something she genuinely deserves. Before he can leave, a sudden flash of light announces [[Chang Wong|Chang]]’s arrival. Chang is in high spirits, eager to share that things with [[Micaela Garcia|Micaela]] have become serious, and Orion is genuinely happy for him. 

Chang gives Orion a thoughtful Christmas gift, a custom-made party board game, and asks in return for a full update on their plans. Orion fills him in on everyone’s holiday whereabouts, the upcoming Ulysses Corp job, and the larger hunt for the remaining Gems of Doom. By the time Chang leaves, Orion feels steadier than he has in days. Even with everything escalating, it helps to know some things, like loyal friends and small moments of normalcy, are still firmly in place.
