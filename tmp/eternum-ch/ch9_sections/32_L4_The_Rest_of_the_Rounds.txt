As the game continued, Orion announced that it was Nancy’s turn, and she confidently chose a faster paced option. The momentum then shifted to Penny, who picked a challenge that tested everyone’s knowledge of the worlds they had traveled through together. One by one, players were eliminated as the pressure mounted, until only Dalia remained standing, claiming a decisive victory that pushed her firmly into the lead.

The next round shifted again, this time favoring Nancy, whose presence alone made the outcome obvious to the group. Even she seemed surprised by how strongly everyone reacted, brushing it off as nothing special while the others struggled to recover. The game moved on quickly, landing back on Dalia, who eagerly chose a challenge that played directly to her strengths. With little debate, the group agreed she had earned the points, and even Dalia’s usual confidence softened into genuine appreciation. As the round ended, attention turned to Orion once more, his next choice already looming as the game showed no signs of slowing down.
