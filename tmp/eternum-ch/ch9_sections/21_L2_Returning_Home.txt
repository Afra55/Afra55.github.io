TITLE: Returning Home
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3862

Orion steadies himself as the world settles back into place. Ekabar. He is back. As he stands, he realizes Calypso was right. His teleportation power has returned, and he can move through Eternum again.

But Hyril’ar is gone from his senses. The absence feels wrong, the same hollow disconnect he felt before, only now it is focused on that single realm. He exhales sharply, frustration creeping in. Calypso always did things her way.

At least one thing is clear. The Gems of Doom matter more than ever. [[The Founder]], [[William Bardot|Bardot]], even Thanatos would come for Hyril’ar and for Calypso’s power. Orion will not let that happen. He will pull her out of that life, no matter what.

For now, all he can do is wait and trust that she can endure what is coming. If anyone can, it is her. With that thought, he turns his focus homeward. He has been gone too long, and he can only hope time has not moved on without him.

Orion exhales as he steps back into Kredon, struck by how good it feels to be home. It feels like he has been gone forever, even though he knows that is not entirely true. His thoughts race ahead to everything he needs to explain and how impossible it feels to even know where to start. Beneath the relief sits a quiet worry that things might have gone badly while he was gone, mixed with a strange hope that they noticed his absence, but not enough to truly suffer because of it.
[[File:The Girls cant find Orion.png|thumb|The Girls cant find Orion]]
As he calls out and moves further inside, he realizes everyone is here. The house is quiet in a way that makes his nerves spike, until he hears voices and understands what he has walked into. They have been searching for him. They have been arguing about leads, about waiting, about how far to go to find him. Hearing his name spoken with fear and frustration makes his chest tighten. 

When he finally reveals himself, the shock is immediate and overwhelming. The silence breaks all at once as everyone rushes him, fear turning into relief and anger and tears all tangled together. They had been terrified. They had counted the hours. They had imagined the worst. Even the strongest among them had been close to breaking, and some already had. Seeing that pain makes it hit Orion all at once just how much his disappearance affected them.

Apologies spill out as he tries to explain, clumsy and rushed, but no one truly needs the details yet. What matters is that he is here. He listens as Nancy tell him what those days were like, how they held on, and how Luna in particular kept everyone standing when it felt impossible. The realization leaves him quiet and heavy with gratitude.

Before anything else, he is urged to go find her. He heads out immediately, moving through familiar streets with his thoughts spinning. Seeing her again stops him in his tracks. Relief turns into urgency as he reaches her and tries to explain everything at once, how he was trapped, powerless, unable to reach them, and how sorry he is for the pain it caused.
[[File:Luna breaks down as she hugs Orion.png|thumb|Luna breaks down as she hugs Orion]]
Luna barely manages to hold herself together. The moment finally cracks, and she breaks down in his arms, admitting how afraid she was and how she doubted even her own visions. Orion holds her tightly, understanding now just how much she carried alone, smiling for everyone else while quietly falling apart herself. 

He soothes her, letting her cry without restraint, telling her she never has to hide herself from him. As the weight finally eases, he admits how much it hurt to see her like that, how deeply he cares, and how much he missed her. Holding her there on the street, he promises her something simple and sincere: that he will never disappear like that again, and that he will never leave her to carry that kind of fear on her own.
