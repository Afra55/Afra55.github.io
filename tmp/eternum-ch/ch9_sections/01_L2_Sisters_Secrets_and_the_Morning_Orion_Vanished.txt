TITLE: Sisters, Secrets, and the Morning Orion Vanished
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2301

After finishing an arm workout, [[Dalia Carter|Dalia]] collapsed onto her bed, muscles burning as she waited for the rain to stop so she could go jogging. [[Penelope Carter|Penny]] burst in soon after, cold and annoyed by the open window. Dalia apologized, explaining the gym was closed for the holiday and she did not want the room to smell. Penny teased her obsession with training but admitted Dalia’s progress was obvious, briefly easing the mood. She then noticed Dalia’s new hair, which Dalia said she had done the day before and forgotten about.
[[File:Penny and Dalia in Dalia's Room.png|thumb|Penny and Dalia in Dalia's Room]]
When Penny suggested they shower together like they used to, Dalia shut it down, clearly uncomfortable. Penny kept teasing until Dalia blurted out that she loved her just to make it stop. Laughing it off, Penny shifted to Eternum, asking about the recent mission. Dalia explained they had discovered [[Hassan Al-Rashid|Hassan]]’s incubation labs, where Xenomorphs were created through sacrifices, and planned to report it so the WRE could wipe everything out. With the secret exposed, she felt confident they would win the event and claim the [[Gem of Doom]]. 

Sensing something off, Penny pressed Dalia about a crush. Dalia denied it until Penny jokingly named Orion, and Dalia’s reaction gave her away. Cornered, Dalia admitted she and Orion had grown close through shared interests and time together. The moment exploded when Penny revealed she had been involved with Orion long before, turning the conversation into a bitter argument fueled by jealousy and old resentment.

They finally agreed the only solution was to confront Orion and force him to choose. They rushed through the house calling for him, only to find his room empty. [[Nancy Carter|Nancy]] appeared, confused, and reminded them it was a holiday. She admitted she was not even sure Orion had come home the night before, which was unusual.
[[File:Dalia and Penny cant find Orion.png|thumb|Dalia and Penny cant find Orion]]
Assuming he was with friends or stuck in Eternum, the sisters split up to look for him, still snapping at each other. Nancy watched them leave, coffee in hand, already exhausted and wondering how she was going to clean up whatever had just exploded between her daughters.
