TITLE: Hope Rekindled in Hyril'ar
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3078

Orion lay half-awake in the sleeping quarters, frustrated by how little control he still had over his transformation. His powers were slowly returning, but he couldn’t hold them for more than a second, leaving him sore, exhausted, and irritated. Daily training was taking its toll, and his body still ached from the previous sparring session. When the general arrived to summon him, Orion groaned at the thought of more drills, briefly wishing he had never insisted on such a relentless schedule.
[[File:Orion hugs Calypso.png|thumb|Orion hugs Calypso ]]
Then, the Princess appeared, and Orion, overwhelmed with relief at seeing her alive, rushed forward and pulled her into a tight hug. Calypso, initially shocked, eventually returned the embrace before pushing him gently away as the general cleared his throat. She reminded him how dangerous such behavior was in a place like this, especially given his state of undress, and Orion apologized, explaining that he had been so surprised to see her that he’d forgotten where he was. 

With the general dismissed for the moment, the Princess explained that she had arranged to accompany Orion during his training so he could finally ask the questions that had been brushed aside. She clarified their location within Hyril’ar, describing the vastness of the kingdom and their journey from the border regions to Iskhaldrim, a fortified settlement nestled deep in the mountains. Orion listened with mild disbelief, joking internally about never remembering all the names, while quietly marveling at how expansive the realm truly was.

Concerned, Orion asked about her condition, relieved to learn that she was steadily recovering after exhausting herself in battle. She recounted waking to the news of his impending execution and regaining her strength just in time to intervene. Hearing this, Orion admitted that he too had been growing stronger since that day, privately wondering if their powers were somehow linked. When he finally asked how he could return to Earth, she revealed that she believed she could open a portal the following day, though not directly to his world. The news filled Orion with renewed hope, as any Eternum server would be enough for him to find his way back.

They spoke briefly of Lorelei, whose forbidden knowledge had saved the Princess’s life and earned her a royal pardon. Orion felt grateful, knowing he owed her more than he could repay. As their conversation drew to a close, the Princess suggested they part ways before training, explaining that being seen alone with a human still carried risks, no matter the truth. She admitted that her own views on humans had once been harsh, but that knowing Orion had changed her perspective.

Left alone, Orion watched her go, his spirits lifted for the first time in days. She was safe, his strength was returning, and for the first time since arriving in Hyril’ar, there was a clear path forward. Tomorrow, he might finally leave this place and reunite with the girls. All he had to do now was survive one more day and not ruin everything before then.
