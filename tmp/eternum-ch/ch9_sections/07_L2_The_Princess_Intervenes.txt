TITLE: The Princess Intervenes
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1803

The guard barked that Orion had been summoned. He got up reluctantly, hoping this might be his chance to defend himself without Lorelei. Changing clothes was unnecessary.
[[File:Orion about to be Hanged.png|thumb|Orion about to be hanged ]]
Escorted by General Arannis and the guards, he was led toward a nearby canyon. He tried to silently plead that the Princess would understand once she woke. Before he could react, two guards grabbed him and dragged him to the noose. The sentence was pronounced: for crimes against Hyril’ar and the Princess, he was to be executed. Orion protested, insisting he was innocent, but the guards cited the law as Regent Sylax and Regent Thorund watched. 
[[File:Calypso stops the execution.png|thumb|Calypso stops the execution]]
His mind raced. Lorelei had said he had three days, yet he now faced death. Struggling against the rope, he prepared to activate his powers. Just as execution seemed inevitable, everything changed. The guards paused. Princess Calypso appeared, commanding attention. The regents bowed in surprise. 

She declared Orion had saved her life and that the others’ judgment had been wrong. The execution was canceled. Instead, he would serve as her personal bodyguard until her recovery. The regents complied, and the guards untied him. Orion exhaled, shaken but alive, another near-death experience behind him.

As preparations for travel began, he noted the organized chaos: sacks loaded, horses saddled, guards briefing. They were retreating to the Usurper’s fortress at the Princess’ command. Orion wouldn’t see her until arrival, but he found comfort knowing she was awake. Twelve hours of carriage lay ahead, surrounded by wary guards, but it was better than the dungeon. He sighed, trusting the girls were safe and capable while he waited.
