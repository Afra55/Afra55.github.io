TITLE: Breaking News
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2444

When Orion finally got back home, he couldn’t stop thinking about how perfect the day had been. The time with Luna, the train ride, even dropping her off at her place all felt unreal. She felt unreal. He kicked off his shoes and called out to the empty house, hoping there was still food left because he was starving, only to notice a note instead. Everyone had gone out and planned to hop on Eternum later that night, though they hadn’t said which server. He considered crashing at Alex’s place or even bringing dessert as a surprise. With over an hour to spare, he told himself he should be productive, grab a snack, read a chapter, do some sit-ups, clean his room, then head out. As always, he reminded himself to squeeze every minute out of life.

He didn’t. Orion passed out on the couch almost immediately. When his phone buzzed, he woke up groggy and annoyed to see it was already past midnight. Another unknown number lit up the screen, and he assumed it was Annie, who had recently changed her phone after going to the UK. Half-asleep, he apologized for missing her calls and explained how he’d planned to surprise everyone but had fallen asleep instead. The voice on the other end told him to turn on the TV. That wasn’t Annie. The caller spoke in vague, unsettling phrases about an unavoidable moment, a Lady, and a path that had already been set, then repeated the demand to turn on the TV before hanging up.
[[File:Orion watching the news .png|thumb|Orion watching the news ]]
Uneasy, Orion stared at the remote, his thoughts racing. Weird things were normal in Eternum, but this was real life. After a few seconds, he turned on the television. The [[News Anchor]] was already calling it the worst technological failure in human history. Reports confirmed a catastrophic malfunction in the neural implant system. Players who died in the simulation or were forcibly disconnected were suffering instant fatal neural feedback in the real world. Ulysses executives blamed a cyberattack while critics accused them of criminal negligence. A global ban on implant usage had been issued, and viewers were warned not to disconnect anyone still logged into Eternum. As the anchor struggled to keep composure, preliminary casualty numbers were announced. The estimated death toll had already climbed past six hundred thousand.

As the Chapter ends, we see Orion, standing in front of the TV, drop his phone in shock.
