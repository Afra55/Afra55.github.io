TITLE: Heading Back Down Stairs
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1865

Orion was still reeling as he headed downstairs, feeling dizzy and trying to pull himself together so he would look normal again. He told himself it was fine, that he just needed food and something to drink, because the hunger was hitting hard. When he reached the dining room, the table was already set, glowing with warmth and decorations that made the whole place feel like a cheesy Christmas movie in the best way, and the smell alone nearly knocked him over.

Alex immediately gave him grief for being late and skipping out on helping set the table, and Orion explained he had stepped outside while chopping wood and ended up taking a call. He admitted that Axel had contacted him out of nowhere and wanted to meet on Thursday night, claiming that William Bardot was behind Benjamin’s death. Alex didn’t trust it at all and made it clear she wanted nothing to do with the Bardots anymore, though she agreed it wouldn’t hurt for Orion to hear Axel out as long as their main plan stayed on track.

Nancy quickly shifted the mood when she announced that dinner was ready, clearly happy to see everyone together. Orion and Alex both admitted that this felt more like a real Christmas than anything they had growing up, warm and personal instead of artificial. Penny was still upstairs, so everyone started getting seated while waiting for her.
[[File:Dalia and her cookies.png|thumb|Dalia and her cookies]]
Before sitting down, Dalia eagerly pulled Orion aside to try the cookies she had been perfecting just for him. Starving, he tried one and was genuinely blown away by how good it was, praising the texture and flavor without holding back. Dalia lit up at his reaction and promised to keep making them, especially since they were packed with protein. With everyone’s stomachs growling, Nancy finally called them all to the table, ready to start dinner at last.
