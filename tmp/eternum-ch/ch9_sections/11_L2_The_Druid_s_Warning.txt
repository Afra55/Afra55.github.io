TITLE: The Druid's Warning
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2619

Calypso greeted [[Lady Briena]] in her chambers, struck by how little the ancient druid had changed despite the years. Briena reacted with open astonishment and reverence, treating Calypso’s return as nothing short of a miracle. Their exchange carried an easy familiarity, shaped by centuries of shared history and Briena’s blunt honesty, a trait Calypso herself had once encouraged.
[[File:Calypso and the Druid.png|thumb|Calypso and the Druid]]
The reason for Briena’s summons soon became clear. She had already anticipated Calypso’s concerns, having sensed the weakening of her magic through the letter sent from the capital. Briena offered her an herbal tea meant to ease the symptoms, explaining that while it would not cure the problem, it would provide temporary relief. Calypso accepted it and was surprised by its pleasant taste, allowing herself a brief moment of comfort before turning to the real issue. 

Calypso described the lingering emptiness she had felt since awakening from her imprisonment. Though physically unharmed, her magic had become sluggish and difficult to control, with even simple acts requiring great effort. Briena listened carefully and revealed that this was not the first time such an affliction had appeared in Calypso’s bloodline. She explained that Calypso’s magic was not fading, but deprived, likening it to deep roots starved of nourishment.

According to Briena, the power of Anima within Calypso was meant to be expressed rather than contained. Suppressing it for too long had caused instability, which now manifested as exhaustion and weakened spellcasting. Calypso needed sex. Though Calypso initially rejected the explanation as absurd and deeply insulting to her dignity, Briena insisted she spoke from knowledge, not superstition, clarifying that balance, not obligation or motherhood, was the core issue.

Briena then revealed that steps had already been taken to protect both Calypso and the realm. A Suitor’s Rite had been arranged at Usurper’s Keep, involving three carefully chosen candidates from powerful houses. The union would stabilize Calypso’s magic and secure fragile alliances at a time when rebellion and foreign threats loomed. Though angered by the decision being made without her consent, Calypso ultimately understood the political necessity.

With visible reluctance, she agreed to attend the rite at dawn and make the choice herself, knowing refusal would be seen as weakness and invite further unrest. Briena, satisfied but not unkind, took her leave to prepare the keep, leaving Calypso alone in silence, burdened by the weight of what lay ahead.
