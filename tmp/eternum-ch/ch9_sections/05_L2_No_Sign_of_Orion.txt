TITLE: No Sign of Orion
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2359

In [[Ekabar]], the search for Orion led the group back to the inn, the last place anyone had seen him. The innkeeper, startled during his morning routine, quickly found himself cornered by [[Alex Bardot|Alex]], who accused him of hiding Orion and Calypso. Despite the threats, the man swore he knew nothing, insisting that after Orion left, half the inn room had burned down and both he and the elf had vanished without a trace. Nancy stepped in to calm things down, reminding Alex that they had already questioned him and that he lacked the power to pull off anything serious. Reluctantly, Alex backed off, though her frustration was clear.

Outside, Nancy reassured Alex that they would find him. Alex admitted her anger came from anxiety, apologizing for losing her temper. Nancy reminded her that Eternum was massive and that five days, while stressful, did not mean the worst. Penny soon returned from questioning the villagers, exhausted and discouraged. No one had seen Orion or Calypso, and the lack of answers was starting to wear on her. Nancy refused to let Penny spiral, insisting that whatever had happened, they were together and alive, even suggesting they could be trapped in a long dungeon or had somehow returned to Hyril’ar.
[[File:Dalia in after looking for Orion in Ekabar.png|thumb|Dalia after looking for Orion in Ekabar]]
Dalia soon joined them after checking the mill. While rumors about the miller being involved in shady dealings proved true, it turned out to be nothing more than drug smuggling, with no sign of Orion or Calypso. Her battered armor raised concern, but she brushed it off, explaining it was old damage and a brief scuffle with the miller’s hired help. Despite insisting she was fine, Dalia made it clear that whoever was responsible for Orion’s disappearance would regret it.  

The group’s attention shifted when Alex spotted smoke rising from Jebediah’s house, one of the first people to see the aftermath from the inn incident. Seeing it as their first real lead, they rushed off together, tension thick in the air. As they moved, Penny and Dalia exchanged sharp looks without speaking, their unresolved conflict hanging between them.

Left briefly alone, Nancy quietly pleaded for Orion to show up soon, knowing that in just five days, the group was already starting to fracture under the strain of his absence.
