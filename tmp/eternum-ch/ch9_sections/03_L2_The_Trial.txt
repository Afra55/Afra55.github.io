TITLE: The Trial
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2305

(For the following, Orion has three optional responses per question. For my own sake, only the recommended choices will be stated.)
[[File:The Council .png|thumb|The Council ]]
Orion began by asking who they were. Thorund introduced himself as the eldest noble of the Realm, Elenwen as the heir of Eldunar, and Sylax as the heir of Cassyr and King’s Hand. They collectively governed Hyril’ar in the Princess Calypso’s absence. Orion was shocked to learn she had vanished fifteen years ago, long before [[Ulysses]] even existed. Thorund instructed him to answer truthfully, warning that any deception would result in immediate execution. 

Orion explained he was the Princess’s sworn protector, having rescued her from the Immortals who held her captive. Elenwen and Sylax doubted him, claiming no human could serve her and that touching her was strictly forbidden by law. Orion insisted he had been genuinely useful and capable, proving himself in combat and in care for the Princess.

Thorund pressed Orion for proof of his loyalty, asking about Hyril’ar’s history. Orion correctly answered questions about [[Anima the Radiant|Queen Anima]], Yliar the Usurper, Calypso’s father Raewyn, her age, and the Elarisil ritual. Thorund nodded, accepting his knowledge as evidence. Thorund’s Daughter then explained the Sirfalas’kadhiel, an ancient trial by sparrow where fate would signal Orion’s innocence or guilt. Orion agreed, allowing the sparrow to choose, leaving the result to the omen.
[[File:Lorelei Testifies .png|thumb|Lorelei Testifies ]]
Before the verdict, Orion requested a witness: [[Lorelei]], General Arannis’ daughter, who had found him with the Princess in the forest. She confirmed his actions were benevolent and his intentions pure. Sylax protested, but Arannis reminded him that Lorelei was noble and one of the Princess’s royal maids. The Council allowed her testimony, after which Orion returned to the tense wait for judgment. 

The Council cast their votes. Sylax and Elenwen found Orion guilty. Thorund, however, declared him not guilty, delaying the final decision until further deliberation. Orion exhaled in relief as the guards returned him to his cell under strict watch. Thorund warned him that this reprieve was not weakness and that the new vote would come after reflection.
