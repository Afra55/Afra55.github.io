A sharp knock rattled Orion’s door. Arannis demanded entry. Panic surged. Calypso froze, whispering in alarm, while Orion’s stomach dropped. There was no time to think. He urged her to hide; pride gave way to urgency. She ducked behind the bed just as Orion opened the door.

Arannis stormed in, scanning the chamber. Orion played the clueless outsider, claiming she had never arrived and he had been waiting for a portal home. Arannis cut him off, insisting Calypso was missing and must go to the Shrine of Lorithien unseen. Lorelei was already on her way. If she didn’t arrive by midnight, they were to flee without him. With that, Arannis left as abruptly as he came.
[[File:Calypso makes Orion swore to secrecy .png|thumb|Calypso makes Orion swear to secrecy ]]
Once the door closed, Calypso emerged, composed and ready. She agreed to go where Arannis instructed. Orion promised to deliver the message before returning to his world. Before parting, they shared a brief, private acknowledgment of what they had shared, swearing the secret would die with them. Calypso left, calling him her knight, leaving Orion rattled but determined. 

He searched the fortress. Corridors echoed with silence—no guards, no servants, kitchens abandoned. Instinct took over as an elven guard lunged from the shadows. Orion’s reflexive burst of power slammed the man to the ground. Recognizing him as one who had tried to string him up, Orion demanded answers. Arannis entered, confirming Orion’s fears: parts of the Royal Guard had turned traitor, threatening Calypso. The Princess was already en route to the shrine, but the traitors might strike through someone she trusted.

Shouts echoed as more guards closed in. Arannis gave Orion a sword but quickly shifted orders. Lorelei mattered more than any stand here. Orion was to slip through the lines and reach Calypso, sending her to Vael’Thalas Hold. Orion hesitated, unwilling to leave Arannis, but was reminded that fighting wasn’t always about swinging a blade. Reluctantly, he obeyed. 

Unleashing his power, Orion vanished, racing toward Calypso. Confusion erupted among the soldiers as Arannis drew their attention. The standoff ended when Regent Sylax appeared, mocking Arannis and offering mercy in exchange for betrayal. Arannis refused, denouncing Sylax’s coup.

Sylax revealed the scope of his treachery: most of the Royal Guard had turned, cities pledged allegiance, and by dawn, a new banner would fly. Arannis warned of the doom this would bring, but Sylax spoke only of rebirth. Surrounded, Arannis stood firm, recounting his lifelong honor, the joy of Lorelei’s birth, and the trust of Calypso’s life. He admitted he never imagined his final stand would shield a human, but he would give his life to deny Sylax victory. As long as Calypso lived, Hyril’ar would never belong to him.

Sylax ordered Arannis’s execution. Surrounded and alone, Arannis remained unbowed.
