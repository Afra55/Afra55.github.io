TITLE: Pushing Limits
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 880

[[File:Orion Pushing his Limits.png|thumb|Orion Pushing his Limits]]
Orion staggered under Arannis’s relentless commands, sweat burning his lungs as his footing slipped and his timing faltered. He cursed through ragged breaths, forcing himself upright and insisting he understood his mistake, begging for another chance. Arannis judged him plainly and called for rest, noting how each attempt slowed him further. Orion refused. He said he was leaving the next day and could not stop now, not while there was still light. When pressed on his stubbornness, he admitted the truth. He needed to be better. He could not fail again. Too much depended on him. Arannis studied him in silence, then smirked and allowed the training to continue. They pushed on until sunset, the commands sharper, the pace faster, Orion answering each with obedience and grit as he fought exhaustion itself.
