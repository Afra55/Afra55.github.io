TITLE: The Suitors Arrive
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3790

Calypso leaned back and questioned Orion about the limits of what he could carry between Eternum and Earth. He explained that items from his world could come with him as long as he was holding them, but objects native to Eternum stayed behind. The Gems were the lone exception, always returning with him no matter where he appeared, for reasons he did not understand. Living beings could cross if he was touching them, though he admitted it was dangerously risky and something he never intended to repeat after bringing Nova, Dalia, and accidentally Alex. When Calypso asked if someone from Eternum could travel to Earth, Orion said he had tried once and failed. Artifacts and people bound to Eternum could not cross over.

Calypso admitted she had sometimes wondered what Earth might be like after hearing him speak of it so often, but dismissed the thought as fleeting. Her duty and life were rooted in Hyril’ar, whether she enjoyed ruling or not. Orion privately reflected that knowing how little she enjoyed her role only made him want to find a way to take her to Earth, even if he had no idea how.

Arannis entered the chamber and bowed, announcing that preparations were complete and the suitors were assembled. Calypso ordered their entry while keeping their delegations at the gates and instructed that most of the guard be assigned to greet them, projecting strength despite stretched forces. After Arannis departed, Orion asked about previous suitors. Calypso explained that Lord Thorund had pressed the issue since her parents’ deaths, but none had been worthy. A king of Hyril’ar had to come from an equal house and excel in battle and the three sacred arts. Orion jokingly measured himself against those standards, but Calypso made it clear that a human courting her would cause outrage and possibly war, though she teasingly allowed that sweet enough music might earn a passing thought.
[[File:Lorelei waves back at Orion.png|thumb|Lorelei waves back at Orion]]
As Calypso’s maids took their seats, Orion noticed Lorelei among them and waved, earning hushed surprise from her side and irritation from the opposite benches. The [[Court Chamberlain]] formally announced the arrival of three suitors and explained the traditions of presentation and deliberation. When the matter of appointing a champion arose, Calypso revealed that Arannis was unavailable and instead named Lord Orion as her champion. Shock rippled through the court, led by Regent Thorund’s outrage. Calypso ended the objections by ordering that Orion’s identity be concealed beneath a helm and declaring her decision final. The Chamberlain reluctantly complied, warning Orion to speak only when permitted and to avoid disgracing the court. 

The suitors were then summoned. [[Fael of Thirinvale]] entered first, graceful and eloquent, praising Calypso’s beauty and listing his ancient lineage and victories. [[Orym of Ilvenmyr]] followed, solemn and honorable, speaking of duty, valor, and the ancient bond between their houses. Finally, [[Torion of Reveil]] strode forward, imposing and blunt, promising powerful heirs and a realm forged in strength. Calypso acknowledged each in turn, maintaining her composure despite the contrast in approaches.
[[File:Sir Grothelborn.png|thumb|Sir Grothelborn]]
With the presentations complete, the Court Chamberlain announced the traditional trials. Orion, now known publicly as Sir Grothelborn of [[Kredon]], stepped forward as champion, feeling the weight of hostile stares but resolving not to fail Calypso. The court declared that the suitors would prove themselves in archery with a longbow, and demonstrations of poetry, painting, and music. As the signal to begin was given, Orion realized just how much more complicated the trials would be than he had expected.
