TITLE: Escaping Eternum for the Holidays
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 4723

Orion hurried to the meeting spot where Nancy was waiting, worried he might be left behind. The thought of spending Christmas Eve alone crossed his mind, but seeing the group in matching outfits quickly lifted his spirits. He apologized for being late, explaining he’d stopped at a café to meet Chang, and Penny assured him it was fine. Nancy admitted she hadn’t prepared anything special this year, but Orion reminded her how much better this felt compared to last year’s lonely Christmas. The plan to spend the night in the mountains sounded perfect, and the group agreed it was a rare chance to unplug from Eternum. Orion noticed Nancy had packed his warmer clothes, since her outfit alone wouldn’t suffice for the mountaintop. He also revealed a custom board game Chang had given him, and everyone was excited to play it later.
[[File:Nancy convinces Alex to go .png|thumb|Nancy convinces Alex to go ]]
Alex arrived next, hesitant to join, but Nancy insisted she was family and had to come. With everyone finally ready, Nancy reminded them to use the restroom before the drive. Penny and Dalia joked and teased over their jackets and hairstyles, their playful banter hinting at a week-long tension. Orion noticed it and Nancy explained it was a normal cycle for them, advising him to act naturally and not intervene. With the last bag loaded, Orion slid into the passenger seat, ready for a memorable Christmas Eve in the mountains. 

Orion and the group finally arrived at the mountain lodge, and Penny was immediately enchanted. Snow blanketed the tall spruces surrounding the cozy wooden house, making it feel like a scene straight out of a Christmas movie. Orion couldn’t help but chuckle at Penny’s excitement, realizing she really loved Christmas. She explained how much this holiday meant to her, and even though gifts were skipped this year, she was thrilled they hadn’t skipped Christmas entirely. Orion admitted he’d never grown up feeling the magic of Christmas, but Penny promised to make all his future Christmases special. Alex, having dozed off for part of the ride, yawned and apologized for passing out, insisting she help with the luggage. Nancy reassured her that it was fine and explained that one bag was refrigerated with ingredients for dinner and kitchen tools, while the other contained Penny’s foldable Christmas tree—because even though they were busy, a tree was essential.
[[File:Dalia tries out the pool.png|thumb|Dalia tries out the pool]]
Dalia gasped at the massive pool with its forest views, and Alex realized why she had packed a bikini. The pool was warm, like a private onsen. Orion complimented the setup while Nancy read the host’s note, revealing the lodge was anti-surveillance and fully secure from Ulysses’ agents. Orion was slightly taken aback by the extreme precautions, but Penny understood why there was no data coverage. Nancy reminded everyone that this could be their last normal weekend before their missions escalated, enforcing a strict “no Eternum” rule—especially for Orion, who could enter without a neural implant. 

Task assignments began: Dalia would help in the kitchen and bake cookies, Penny would prepare the beds and fireplace, Alex would decorate the tree, and Orion was on lumberjack duty, responsible for chopping logs and getting the fire going. Orion accepted his role, shivering slightly as he grabbed his jacket, ready to play lumberjack and contribute to the festive preparations. The girls dispersed with excitement, and dinner was set for six, with Nancy insisting they stay hungry until then.

Orion focused on chopping firewood, counting each log as he worked and stopping once he was confident there were enough to keep the fire going all night. As he finished, he couldn’t help but notice how easily the axe split each log clean in half, realizing that it wasn’t just practice or technique. He had genuinely gotten much stronger. Satisfied with the work, he prepared to head back inside and considered checking if Nancy and Dalia needed help in the kitchen. Before he could move on, his phone buzzed, surprising him since there was supposed to be no signal. He realized he must have stepped just far enough beyond the perimeter to reconnect and saw a message from Annie, who was excited to show him that it was snowing in London. They exchanged a few lighthearted messages, joked about the sudden signal, and said goodbye before she logged off. Orion smiled to himself, thinking about how adorable she was and feeling glad he’d been able to reply at all. Just as he assumed the signal would drop again, an unknown number started calling his phone, leaving him unsure whether to ignore it as spam or answer before losing reception entirely.
