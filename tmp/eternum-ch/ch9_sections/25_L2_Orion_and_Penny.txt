TITLE: Orion and Penny
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3746

[[File:Why Penny became a girl scout.png|thumb|Why Penny became a girl scout ]]
Orion carried as many logs as he could manage, hauling them back to the house and dropping them by the door. Hunger gnawed at him, but the warmth from the fireplace immediately enveloped him as he stepped inside, and he finally began to feel the Christmas spirit. Penny greeted him, relieved he hadn’t left the mountain bare. Orion explained it had taken a while to find decent dry wood, joking about his expertise, and mentioned he’d been delayed by a phone call from Axel Bardot, who wanted to meet on Thursday. Penny agreed that talking to Alex first was a good idea, but they both decided the call could wait—tonight was a detox from Eternum. They admired the Christmas tree and the fire, with Orion impressed that Penny had built it herself using dry timber and steel knives, revealing she was a five-time Best Girl Scout winner. She had learned first aid to look out for Dalia and other friends, and Orion chuckled at how caring she was. 

Penny, feeling unmotivated, decided to help in the kitchen instead of working on her journalism capstone, which involved researching a topic and conducting interviews. Orion suggested exposing [[Mr. Valentino|Nico Valentino]], a manipulative figure from Kredon, which Penny loved as a potential topic for her project. Nancy checked in, confirming dinner would be ready in about forty minutes, while Dalia and Alex worked on the cookies—though Dalia groaned at their quality. Orion relaxed by the fire, aware that Nancy’s soundproofed walls made it a perfect time for private conversations. Taking a deep breath, he prepared to talk to Penny about a complicated topic. As he is starting the conversation, Penny cuts him off asking if he would run up stairs with her so she can pick a bikini top. Orion accepts and brings the logs in before joining her. 
[[File:Penny on the bed.png|thumb|Penny on the bed ]]
Orion brought the logs inside and stacked them by the fire before heading upstairs. He found the first room on the left, where Penny was sitting on the floor, distracted by a small cabinet. The room was clearly the best in the house, warm and softly lit, and Penny had claimed it fast. They sat together on the bed, and what started as light teasing quickly shifted into something more serious as Penny asked for honesty, making it clear she needed the truth from him.  
[[File:Penny reveals Akira's lie detector .png|thumb|Penny reveals Akira's lie detector  ]]
She pressed him about his feelings and whether he had been with anyone else since the studio, refusing to let him dodge the questions. Orion admitted the truth, including his feelings for Dalia, and then confessed that he loved Penny too. The tension broke when Penny revealed she had secretly used [[Akira]]'s lie detector to make sure he was being honest, and it confirmed that he was. The weight she had been carrying finally lifted, and she admitted she had already worked through the shock and confusion while he was gone.

They talked openly about how everything had come out, how Nancy had tried to protect the situation, and how Penny ultimately decided that if Orion’s feelings were real, it was better not to force things or rush them. The conversation turned lighter again, filled with teasing and warmth, and the fear Orion had been carrying faded as he realized Penny understood him more than he had hoped.

Before heading back downstairs, curiosity got the better of them. Orion managed to open the locked cabinet Penny had been fiddling with, revealing some very questionable belongings left behind by the owner of the house. The two decide to use these items and have a little fun, heading into Orion and Penny's fourth romance scene.
