TITLE: Shelter from the Storm
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3427

Orion and Luna stepped off the train into the deserted rural station and followed a narrow path toward the old estate grounds, still flooded from recent rains. The house looked mysterious, and while Luna wished they had more time to explore, Orion was quietly relieved they did not. The isolation stood out to him, with nothing but the rail line and scattered, lonely houses stretching across the region. Nearby, another couple argued loudly about pictures and wanting to leave, their bickering breaking the quiet.

Luna joked darkly about the murky water around the house, which made Orion pause before suggesting they take a picture. Luna immediately brightened, rushing to pose in front of the house with childlike excitement. Orion took a photo, struck by how she made even the creepiest place feel warm. Just as they talked about taking one together, the rain suddenly poured down. With no better option, they ran for the porch and slipped inside the abandoned house as the wind forced the door open behind them.

Inside, the cold set in fast. Orion searched for anything they could use to get back to the train while Luna tried to dry off, both half joking about ghosts. The house was mostly empty, filled with dust and rotting boards, though Orion managed to get an old generator running. He was surprised by how calm he felt, realizing that with Luna there, even a haunted house did not feel frightening.

In the basement, Orion found an old ceremonial mask and decided to try scaring Luna in playful revenge. His plan backfired when Luna used a trapdoor to sneak up behind him and scare him far worse than he intended. They both laughed hard, and Orion accepted that he would always be the one getting jumpscared, noticing how free and joyful Luna seemed.
[[File:Luna's reaction to hearing Orion say te amo.png|thumb|Luna's reaction to hearing Orion say te amo]]
When Luna worried she had been annoying and apologized for laughing so much, Orion reassured her, telling her there was nothing wrong with her and that being different was a good thing. Luna admitted she was still learning how to stop holding herself back now that she felt happier and more open. Orion told her she had changed for the better while still being the same kind, sweet, wonderfully strange person he cared about. Overcome, he told her how much she meant to him and  added ''te amo'', the words slipping out before he could overthink them. After a brief pause, Luna kissed him, ending his nervous doubt. 

They held each other quietly, the moment uninterrupted. Luna admitted she did not want to regret holding back, and Orion welcomed it, staying close as the rest of the world faded away around them. The two then share their third romance scene.

Orion and Luna’s train ride back was filled with easy laughter and quiet moments, and soon they found themselves at the bus stop in Kredon. Orion couldn’t believe the day was already over, but reminded her it was only the first of many dates. Luna laughed, and the two shared a soft, sincere exchange of “I love you.”  

When the bus arrived, Luna assured him the ride home was short and promised to see him later in Eternum. She boarded, and Orion watched the bus disappear down the street. Alone, he thought about how perfect the day had been and how lucky he felt, then decided to head home, but not before heading to get some crepes.
