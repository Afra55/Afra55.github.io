TITLE: Using her Power for Good
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3084

As the train crossed a bridge, Luna quietly watched the scenery until the [[Train Conductor]] announced a brief clearing in the rain and pointed out a perfect view of Mount Baker. Luna hurried into the corridor to look, leaning toward an open window in awe as the clouds parted. While she was distracted, an irritated passenger tried to force his way past her in the narrow space. Startled, Luna apologized as he shoved by, and the brief contact was enough to trigger one of her visions. In an instant, she saw that the man was named [[Rowan]], and the vision revealed his explosive anger and the abuse he inflicted on his wife, [[Tessa]]. When it ended, Rowan dismissed her reaction and walked away, leaving Luna shaken and frozen in place.

Orion returned from the bathroom moments later, relieved that the rain had eased, and suggested continuing the movie. When he noticed Luna had put her gloves back on, he immediately sensed something was wrong. After some hesitation, Luna explained that Rowan, the same man who had bumped into her in the corridor, had triggered a vision showing him hurting his wife. Orion was furious and worried but focused on supporting Luna, reminding her that her visions were not her fault. When Luna said the future she saw could not be changed, Orion pushed back, pointing out that her powers did not work on him and suggesting they might be able to intervene together. After thinking it through, Luna agreed to try scaring Rowan into stopping, using what she had seen and a quick disguise. When the train entered a long tunnel and the lights flickered, they set their plan in motion.
[[File:Luna in her disguise .png|thumb|Luna in her disguise ]]
In the darkness of the carriage, Rowan grew increasingly unsettled as strange sounds and flickering lights surrounded him. Luna appeared before him, transformed and unrecognizable, confronting him with details only he could know. Terrified, Rowan broke down and promised to leave his wife, sign the divorce papers, and never return. As the train exited the tunnel, Rowan fled in a panic, shoving past other passengers as he ran back to his seat. Orion and Luna slipped back into their cabin just in time, hiding the disguise as Luna realized it had worked. Overwhelmed with relief, she was amazed she had used her powers to protect someone, and Orion reassured her, proud and steady, reminding her that this part of her did not need to be hidden. 

Caught up in the moment, they grew close and shared a passionate kiss until the conductor cleared her throat, politely apologizing for the interruption and letting them know the next stop was only minutes away. She mentioned a nearby abandoned house with a spooky reputation and suggested it was the perfect chance for photos. Intrigued, Luna immediately wanted to go, thrilled by the idea of exploring something haunted. Orion admitted he had planned it as a surprise and warned her it was probably just an old house with a legend, though he agreed to check it out. Eager not to miss their chance, Luna insisted they leave as soon as the train stopped.
