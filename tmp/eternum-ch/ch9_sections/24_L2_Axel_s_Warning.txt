TITLE: Axel's Warning
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1598

Orion answered the call and was immediately met with frustration from the person on the other end, who turned out to be Axel. Axel asked if he was with Alex and explained that he couldn’t reach her either. Orion confirmed they were together in the mountains and pointed out the lack of signal, pressing Axel on why he was calling now after weeks of silence. Axel then dropped the reason for the call, claiming his father had killed Benjamin. Orion was stunned and pushed back, insisting a Praetorian was responsible and that the Founder controlled them, not William. Axel countered by saying his father controlled enough of them and claimed to know what was being planned, though he refused to explain over the phone. He insisted they meet in person. After some back and forth about timing, they agreed on Thursday evening at the park by the station. Before hanging up, Axel warned Orion not to approach his father under any circumstances and to stay out of it until they talked, stressing how dangerous he was.

Once the call ended, Orion pocketed his phone, uneasy and deeply suspicious of Axel’s motives. He couldn’t shake the feeling that Axel might be lying or working for his father, though he admitted it might still be worth hearing him out. There was no chance he could mention their plans to dig through William’s office on Wednesday without talking to the others first, especially Alex. Pushing the thoughts aside, Orion turned his attention back to the task at hand and went to gather the firewood, determined not to be the reason the fire went out and ruined Dalia’s marshmallow plans.
