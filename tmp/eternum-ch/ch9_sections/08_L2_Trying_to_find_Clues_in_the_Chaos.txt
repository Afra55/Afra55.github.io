TITLE: Trying to find Clues in the Chaos
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2609

[[Sandra Johnson|Sandra]] knocked gently on [[Nova Johnson|Nova]]’s door, then again, growing more insistent as there was no response. Inside, Nova was half lost in thought, muttering to herself while scribbling and staring at her screen, completely absorbed in patterns, numbers, and theories that refused to line up. Sandra sighed, called her name louder, and finally mentioned that [[Annie Winters|Annie]] was there to see her. That snapped Nova out of her spiral just enough to recognize the name. She told Sandra to let Annie in, already drifting back into her thoughts as Sandra stepped away, quietly hoping the visit might help lift Nova’s mood after the last few days. 
[[File:Annie has a depressing aura.png|thumb|Annie has a depressing aura]]
In the hallway, Sandra tried to make polite conversation with Annie, worried about Orion. She suggested he might be visiting family or lost in Eternum, but Annie shut that down—Orion had no family, and Eternum was dangerous. Sandra, out of her depth, offered to let the police handle it, unaware that Eternum’s rules made that impossible, and then left for Nova’s room.

Inside, Annie apologized for being late, exhausted. Nova barely registered it, fixated on a realization about dates and formats. She announced she had cracked everything and claimed to know exactly where Orion was. Her explanation spiraled wildly, covering ciphers, conspiracies, Calypso, Ulysses, and Eternum’s core server, eventually pointing to Central Africa. Annie struggled to keep up, questioning whether Nova had slept at all.
[[File:Nova and her Board.png|thumb|Nova and her Board]]
When Nova insisted they needed to travel to Africa, Annie broke down. She admitted she didn’t believe Orion was there and confessed she had no idea where he was. Overwhelmed, she voiced her fear that he might be gone for good. Nova, frustrated but determined, pressed on with logic and hacking plans, refusing to give up. 

[[Luna Hernandez|Luna]]’s calm voice finally grounded them. She said with certainty that Orion was okay and would return, trusting his strength and that he would never abandon them. Slowly, tension eased. Annie wiped her tears, acknowledging Luna was right, and Nova forced a laugh, embarrassed by her spiral. They shared a small laugh, grateful for Luna’s steadiness.

Nova snapped back into energy, insisting it was time to regroup and approach the search with clearer heads. They scrapped the Africa theory and brainstormed new possibilities, including that Orion could be held by ERE remnants on another server. For the first time in days, panic gave way to a plan.
