TITLE: Training Session
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2830

[[File:Arannis and Lorelei.png|thumb|Arannis and Lorelei]]
Orion paused at the crossroads to take in the sweeping valley, struck once again by its quiet beauty and how unreal it felt, even after days of staring at it. As he continued on, he soon encountered General Arannis and Lady Lorelei, thanking them for escorting Calypso earlier and expressing how her presence had lifted his spirits. General Arannis, stern as ever, made it clear that while he would respect Calypso’s wishes, Orion’s role came with strict boundaries and grave consequences should he ever overstep them. Orion accepted the warning without hesitation. 

Calypso and Lorelei soon joined them, exchanging warm pleasantries that showed their long shared history. General Arannis voiced concern about Calypso’s striking appearance being a liability, but she dismissed it, confident in the valley’s seclusion and her own abilities. As they walked, the emptiness of the valley became apparent, prompting an explanation that the people and soldiers had been relocated due to growing tensions and declining birthrates. Calypso accepted this grim reality with resolve, already thinking ahead to what she would need to repair once fully recovered.
[[File:The big Gates.png|thumb|The Massive Doors]]
Their path led them to an ancient hidden temple, older than elves or humans, where Orion noticed a familiar symbol carved into the massive doors: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouroboros the Ouroboros]. The realization that a symbol from his own world existed here unsettled him deeply and raised troubling questions about ancient travelers and lost connections between worlds. Despite his curiosity, the sealed doors could not be opened, and the group instead prepared for training nearby. 
[[File:The start of Training.png|thumb|The start of Training]]
General Arannis pushed Orion harder than before, replacing wooden practice blades with real steel and showing no mercy in his approach. Fear and doubt surfaced briefly, but when pressed about what he would do to protect those he cared for, Orion found his resolve. Their clash was intense, ending with Orion’s blade poised at General Arannis’s throat. Though impressed, the general reminded him that real enemies would show no restraint and demanded even greater focus and speed. 

As training continued, Calypso and Lorelei observed from the sidelines, openly impressed by Orion’s strength, stamina, and rapid growth. Their conversation drifted into curiosity about humans and Orion’s companions, until Lorelei’s unchecked fascination crossed into awkward territory, earning a sharp rebuke from Calypso. The moment was cut short by news of a druid’s arrival, drawing Calypso away for urgent matters. She departed with composed grace, leaving Orion to refocus on his training under General Arannis’s watchful eye.
