TITLE: The Rest of the Rounds
LEVEL: 4
CHARS: 2084

Orion was fired up, calling out “Alright, alright, alright! Nancy! Smol but Mighty… or Picky Pack?” Nancy hesitated, then chose Smol but Mighty. The next card was a group challenge: the shortest King or Queen in the group would earn 100 points. Dalia immediately claimed it since Annie and Nova weren’t there, but Penny argued Alex might be shorter. Nancy spotted a measuring tape by the garden and suggested they settle it for real. Dalia and Alex hopped out of the pool, complaining about the cold, while Nancy measured them. Dalia came in at 5’5”, perfectly average, while Alex was an inch shorter at 5’4”, much to everyone’s amusement. Alex protested being called short and cute, while Orion laughed at how flustered she got.

The game continued heating up. Nancy prompted Penny to choose between Heavy Fireworks or Most Wanted Sandwich. Penny picked Sandwich, which led to a team-building card: each player had to vote on which two people in the group they’d choose for a threesome. Dalia, Nancy, and Penny all voted for Orion and Alex, while Alex picked Orion and Dalia. Orion chose Alex and Dalia as well, resulting in a tie and 100 points for each.

Next, Penny’s “Burn, Baby, Burn” card led to a plank duel between Orion and Dalia. They dropped into position, joking and teasing each other about who could last longer. Despite the cold, neither gave up, counting seconds and trading playful taunts. Six minutes and thirteen seconds later, Dalia finally admitted defeat, panting and conceding victory to Orion.

It was Dalia’s turn again with a choice between Movie Mime and Fast and Furious. She picked Movie Mime, which turned into a silent movie-guessing game with Penny. Dalia whispered six classic movies to Alex, and Penny guessed them all instantly: Titanic, The Matrix, Inglourious Basterds, Pulp Fiction, The Fellowship of the Ring, and Ratatouille. Everyone was stunned at how fast she nailed them.

Finally, they reached the last round. Orion braced himself as Dalia presented his final choice: X Marks the Spot or Bribe Me Baby. He paused, considering his options.
