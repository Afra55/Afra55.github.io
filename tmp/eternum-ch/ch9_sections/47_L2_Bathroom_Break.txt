TITLE: Bathroom Break
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2078

A few minutes later, Orion and Luna quietly reacted to the movie, wondering whether a character was truly alive or if it was meant to represent a descent into madness. Orion agreed with her interpretation and admitted that if he were in that situation, he would probably lose his mind too, especially if it involved her. Luna teased him and said she would simply find a way to bring him back, convinced that in a world with eldritch horrors, resurrection had to be possible. Orion found that very on brand for her and laughed. Needing a bathroom break before the plot thickened, he paused the movie and stepped out, promising to return quickly while Luna stayed behind with the hot chocolate.
[[File:Noah and Giuseppe.png|thumb|Noah and Giuseppe]]
As Orion walked down the narrow corridor, he reflected on how perfectly the trip was going and how the rain, which he once hated, now felt magical. When he reached the bathroom, he found it occupied and waited, growing increasingly uncomfortable. While standing there, he noticed [[Noah and Raul|Noah]] nearby, surprisingly separated from [[Noah and Raul|Raul]], and speaking with a man he recognized from earlier. Curious, Orion listened as [[Giuseppe Bellini]] spoke at length about his childhood dog, Nero, describing its strength, loyalty, and eventual decline. He recounted how his father had put the dog down out of mercy and explained that love sometimes takes cruel forms for the sake of what comes next. The story clearly carried a deeper meaning, and when the man asked if Noah understood, Noah eventually agreed. After a moment of silence, Noah shook the man’s hand, seemingly accepting some kind of deal, along with the idea of stepping out of another person’s shadow. 

The moment was broken when another passenger snapped at Orion for blocking the tight corridor. Orion moved aside, annoyed by the hostility but determined not to let it ruin his mood. As the man stormed off, Orion shook it off, reminding himself that nothing was going to spoil the day, even as the need for the bathroom became increasingly urgent.
