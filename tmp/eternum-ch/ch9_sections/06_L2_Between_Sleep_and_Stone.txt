TITLE: Between Sleep and Stone
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1886

[[File:Idriel appears in the cell.png|thumb|Idriel appears in the cell]]
Orion slowly opened his eyes, groaning as a sharp headache tore through his skull. Sleeping on the cold, wet stones without a pillow had done nothing to help, and he felt every nerve screaming in protest. His head felt like it was about to split open, and he briefly considered asking the guards for a painkiller, though he knew it would be useless. As he tried to gather his thoughts, a strange presence filled his mind, and he immediately recognized it—[[Idriel]]. Panic surged through him; he didn’t want to see or hear anything from this entity. Idriel’s voice questioned the nature of reality, asking whether something is real if it can be felt, even in dreams, memories, or fear. Orion refused to engage, insisting he didn’t care and demanding the voice leave. 

Idriel laughed softly, insisting they weren’t so different, bound by the same chain. Orion recalled the times he had seen Idriel before—when he was shot in an alley and again when [[Thanatos]] nearly killed him at the inn—and wondered why these visions kept haunting him without offering any answers. Idriel offered a story, and despite Orion’s resistance, he reluctantly agreed to listen.

The story described an ancient door, worn smooth by countless hands, and a man who stood before it, knocking repeatedly. The door remained shut despite his efforts, and the man turned to leave, only to be urged to try once more. On this final attempt, the door finally opened. Orion understood that something had changed in the man himself, not the door, though Idriel remained enigmatic about the specifics. When Orion asked what was behind the door, Idriel only said it was a good question, leaving the answer unspoken. Just then, a knock at Orion’s cell door pulled him out of the lingering dream, jolting him back to the cold reality of the dungeon.
