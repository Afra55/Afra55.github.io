TITLE: The Heist
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 3073

[[File:Undercover Orion.png|thumb|Undercover Orion]]
His phone buzzed with confirmation from Chang that Operation Plug’n’Pray was ready. Chang and Mia were in position, using Elevator 7 between floors 2 and 14. Orion acknowledged the signal and headed for the front desk, presenting himself as Alek Claimant, a candidate for Division Director of Advanced Research. After a brief ID check, security cleared him to proceed. Elevator 7 felt oddly reassuring, with no cameras in sight. 
[[File:Orion, Luna, and Chang in the elevator .png|thumb|Orion, Luna, and Chang in the elevator ]]
Chang joined him briefly on floor 2, immediately panicking over Orion’s missing eyepatch. The cover identity required full commitment, not half measures. Orion scrambled to put it on just in time before the elevator doors opened again. When Luna stepped in on floor 14, flawlessly disguised in professional attire, Orion nearly forgot the tension of the mission. She chatted casually, complimenting him and handing Chang a bag of homemade sweets she had prepared for later. Chang was visibly stunned by the gesture. Luna exited on floor 25, wishing them luck as she headed to her own interview, where she would trigger the planned blackout. 

Orion and Chang continued to floor 28 and separated near HR, careful not to be seen together. Chang reminded him that from this point on, Orion Richards no longer existed. He was Alek Claimant now. The goal was simple: distract [[Adrian Powell]] long enough for the power to cut, plant Nova’s USB, and disappear without a trace.

Nearby, Luna waited among other applicants. One of them, Ian Abelman, casually explained Ulysses’ interview strategy, emphasizing that competition was encouraged and hesitation punished. When he asked Luna what role she was applying for, she calmly stated she wasn’t there for a job at all. She revealed that William Bardot was in the building, that Ulysses had destroyed her family, and that she intended to kill him. The man fled in terror, drawing attention as interviews for the Regional Marketing Manager position were called. Luna stood when summoned, unfazed by the chaos she had just caused.
[[File:Adrian Powell calls Orion and Chang in for the interview .png|thumb|Adrian Powell calls Orion and Chang in for the interview ]]
Back in the waiting area for Advanced Research, Orion counted the remaining candidates and checked the time. Everything was lining up. Adrian Powell burst into the room with overwhelming energy, immediately establishing himself as loud, erratic, and unpredictable. He announced that only one applicant would earn the position and wasted no time provoking, joking, and testing reactions. When Alek Claimant was called, Orion stood, eyepatch in place, playing the role perfectly as Powell fixated on his appearance and physical presence. 

As Orion followed Powell into the office alongside Roberto Canchez, he focused on one thing only. Luna should be starting her interview now. If they could last fifteen minutes without blowing their cover, the mission would succeed.

This was it.
