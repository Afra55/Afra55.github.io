TITLE: The Rest of the Rounds
LEVEL: 4
CHARS: 2537

Orion was riding the high of the game, feeling like victory was within reach as he called out to Nancy. Dalia urged him to speed things up so the winner could finally get to the sauna, and Orion presented her with the choice between Crowned and Honored or Size Does Matter. Nancy giggled, curious about what Size Does Matter involved. The card revealed a group challenge: whoever had seen the biggest penis in real life would earn 100 points, not counting their own. The group reacted with awkward hesitation, but Alex confidently jumped in, describing a past encounter in vivid detail that left everyone stunned. Nancy admitted that while she had seen an impressive one as well, Alex’s description was unbeatable, giving her the round. Dalia quickly moved on, eager to finish the conversation.
[[File:Alex discovers everything.png|thumb|Alex discovers everything]]
They reached the next round, where Penny chose Dice of Doom. Everyone rolled, and Dalia and Orion were unlucky, having to leave the pool for two minutes while still keeping score. As they waited, Penny prompted Alex to pick between Saint Among Sinners and Basketball Star, and she chose the former. The card rewarded 100 points to anyone who had never had a wild night with anyone in the room, but required finishing a drink. Alex said she hadn’t, and the group clarified that Orion and Dalia should count too, though no points were earned in the end, Penny confronted Alex about knowing about her and Orion while also letting it slip that she and Orion have done some things too. The two look over at Nancy who did not touch her drink. Penny is in shock that Nancy and Orion have also done things. Alex exclaims that this family was wild. Just then, Orion and Dalia’s two-minute pool exile ended, they prepared for the final showdown. 

For the last round, Alex presented Dalia with her final two options: Big Brain Smoothie or Coin of Chaos. Dalia chose Coin of Chaos, which involved a duel: she and Orion each picked a side of a coin and tossed it to see who would earn 100 points, worth triple for the final score. Dalia trusted her gut and chose tails, leaving Orion with heads. When the coin landed, it came up Heads—Orion had won. Orion chose to say it came up tails however. Dalia celebrated as the official winner, claiming the sauna and inviting Alex to join her. Penny congratulated her before heading to bed, with Nancy helping her gather some "toothpaste". Orion chuckled at the outcome and the chaos of the night, while Dalia and Alex enjoyed their victory.
