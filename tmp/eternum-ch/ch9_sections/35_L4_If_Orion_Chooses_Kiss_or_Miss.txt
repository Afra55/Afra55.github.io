TITLE: If Orion Chooses Kiss or Miss
LEVEL: 4
CHARS: 650

Orion chose the next option. Dalia read it out and it's a guessing game. Orion covered his eyes while the group whispered and conspired about which one of them would kiss him. The anticipation alone made him grin, and when someone finally moved closer, the brief kiss was far more confident and playful than he had expected. By the time it ended, he was impressed, and when he opened his eyes, the group waited to see if he could figure it out. Trusting his instincts, he made his guess, and Alex’s reaction confirmed he was right. She admitted it with a mix of teasing and pride, and the game rolled on with the energy noticeably higher than before.
