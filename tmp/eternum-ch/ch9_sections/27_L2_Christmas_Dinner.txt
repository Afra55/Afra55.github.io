TITLE: Christmas Dinner
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 2204

The spread looked incredible, with juicy turkey, glazed ham, and sides that smelled even better than they had in the kitchen. Orion couldn’t help but admire it all and give Nancy credit, though she brushed it off and insisted they wait until Penny arrived. Moments later, Penny came rushing downstairs, instantly overwhelmed by how perfect everything looked. To her, the table felt straight out of a Christmas commercial, and her excitement was impossible to miss.

Before sitting down, Penny pulled Orion aside for a brief, private moment. She leaned in close and quietly returned the words he had shared earlier, telling him she loved him too. After that, she quickly rejoined the group, clearly eager to eat. Orion could not help smiling to himself as Dalia immediately noticed the secrecy and poked fun at her sister for it. Penny brushed it off, firing back with her usual sharp attitude.

As soon as Penny sat down, she jumped back up in discomfort, trying to play it off while Nancy worried over her. Penny awkwardly blamed it on spicy food, insisting she was fine and just needed a moment. Nancy gave Orion a knowing look. Dalia, of course, showed no mercy, teasing Penny relentlessly and turning it into a loud sibling back-and-forth that ended with mock threats over cookies and insults flying both ways.
[[File:Orion shares a toast.png|thumb|Orion shares a toast]]
Once things settled, Nancy asked Orion to raise a toast. Caught off guard, he still stood and spoke honestly from the heart. He admitted that Christmas had never meant much to him before this year, but now he finally understood. It was not about gifts or decorations, but about being together. He spoke about choosing these people, about wanting a life where they were safe and happy, no matter what missions or dangers waited ahead. He promised to fight for that future and ended by toasting to their shared memories and the ones still to come. 

The table erupted in cheers. Alex and Dalia were stunned, Nancy was openly emotional, and Penny was clearly touched. Orion laughed it off when they teased him about secretly rehearsing, but the moment lingered warmly as Nancy finally told everyone to eat before the food got cold.
