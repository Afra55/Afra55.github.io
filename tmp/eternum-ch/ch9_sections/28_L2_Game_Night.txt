A few hours later, the five of them made their way outside to the heated pool, steam rolling into the cold night air as snow surrounded them. The moment Orion slipped into the water, the tension drained out of his body. The contrast between the freezing air and the hot water felt unreal, and for a moment, everything else faded away. He sat there quietly, full, relaxed, and oddly at peace, thinking about how this Christmas had already surpassed every other one he could remember.

That calm did not last long. Dalia hesitated at the edge of the pool, shivering and complaining about the cold while Orion tried, unsuccessfully, to keep his thoughts in check as she stepped closer. Once she finally lowered herself into the water, the shock melted into relief, and her reaction made it clear the heat was worth it. Orion leaned back, trying to act normal, but Alex immediately caught on, calling him out the moment she joined them in the pool. She made a show of teasing him before diving in herself, quickly admitting that the water really was hot.

Nancy arrived next, distracted by the bright moon and the way the snow made the mountains glow, completely unfazed by the cold. Penny followed close behind, clearly freezing and eager to get into the water as fast as possible. The instant she eased herself in, her reaction mirrored everyone else’s relief, the warmth cutting straight through the chill. Soon, all five of them were settled, the steam rising around them as the night grew quieter.

Nancy then pulled out a card game they had brought, explaining the rules while everyone listened with growing interest. The game promised chaos, competition, and plenty of chances to prove themselves, with points, challenges, and a final round that could flip everything upside down. Since they were all exhausted and broke, the prize became the real debate, until Alex suggested that the winner get first dibs on the sauna before bed. That immediately sealed the deal. 

Drinks were passed around, confidence levels rose, and Dalia volunteered to start, clearly determined to win. Orion played it cool, joking about luck and rules, but made it clear he had no intention of backing down. As Dalia drew the first two cards and read out the options, the game officially began, setting the tone for a long, competitive night in the steaming pool.
