TITLE: If Orion Chooses X Marks the Spot
LEVEL: 4
CHARS: 733

Orion shouted, “X Marks the Spot!” and Dalia got ready. The next card was a duel: whoever read it had to kiss the other wherever they chose, or the challenger got the points if they chickened out. Orion was excited, and Dalia accused him of always getting the kissing cards and maybe cheating. Orion laughed it off. When Dalia asked where he wanted the kiss, Orion chose the lips. Surprised, Dalia grumbled but finally agreed, reminding herself it was just a party game. She straddled his lap and whispered that he hadn’t expected her to do it in front of everyone. Orion whispered back that he had hoped she would. Dalia leaned in and kissed him softly. The others reacted with surprise, but Dalia quickly called for the next round.
