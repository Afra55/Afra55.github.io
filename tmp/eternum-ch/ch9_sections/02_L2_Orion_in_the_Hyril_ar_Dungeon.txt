TITLE: Orion in the Hyril’ar Dungeon
LEVEL: 2
CHARS: 1939

A steady thud echoed as Orion’s fist struck the cold stone wall. His powers—teleportation, berserk strength—were gone, leaving him cut off and frustrated. Days in the dungeon blurred without sunlight.

Nearby, elven guards joked about rats and the Princess’s return. Their crude admiration made Orion tense; their tone was nothing like [[Calypso]]’s. From their chatter, he learned she had been recovered and might prevent war.
[[File:Orion in the Dungeon .png|thumb|Orion in the Dungeon  ]]
Desperate, Orion asked about Calypso. The guards responded with hostility, confirming she was alive but unconscious, blaming him. His denials meant nothing. Left alone, he sank to the floor as the guards reminisced about a human imprisoned centuries ago—until [[Regent Sylax]] arrived. 

Sylax entered with disdain, questioning Orion’s origins and connection to the Princess. He dismissed Orion’s protests and informed him that his trial before the Council awaited. Dumping a sack on the floor, Sylax ordered him to eat and change, mocking his pride when he refused.

Alone, Orion focused. This trial was dangerous, but it was also his only chance. Changing into ornate elven robes, he was shackled and escorted out, grateful for the sunlight by the lake.

Inside the Council hall, [[General Arannis]]’s cold indifference unsettled him. [[Regent Elenwen]] argued over [[Thorund’s Daughter]]’s presence until [[Regent Thorund]] confirmed she had been summoned as his successor.

Summoned forward, Orion tried to speak, insisting he had not harmed the Princess, but Thorund silenced him. He was charged with trespassing sacred land, violating ancient law, and laying hands on the Princess, who had been found near death in his presence. With no human entering [[Hyril'ar]] for centuries, the case threatened the Realm’s stability.

The trial began in secrecy, and Orion realized survival depended on answering carefully and speaking only when asked.
